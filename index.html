How to use Nibill
① People
Type a name and initials for everyone at the table, then tap Add Person. Initials fill in automatically — edit them if needed. Optionally set a currency, date and restaurant name.
② Bill Total
Enter the final amount from the receipt. This is used to track a running balance as you add items, and to split any gap equally at the end.
③ Items
Add each line from the bill. As soon as you add an item, a panel appears to assign it to people.

Assigning: Tick everyone who shared the item. Leave the value blank for an equal split. Or enter a % or £ amount per person — tap the toggle to switch, and values auto-convert.

Editing: Use ✎ to change the name or price, and 👥 to change who it's assigned to.

The dark bar at the top shows how much of the bill you've entered and what's still to allocate. Any unallocated remainder is split equally across everyone at the end.
④ Tip
Choose a service charge percentage, or type a custom one. It's split proportionally based on each person's share of the bill.
⑤ Summary
Shows what each person owes with a full itemised breakdown. Tap ▲ Collapse to show totals only.

Save as image downloads a PNG of the summary — great for sharing to a WhatsApp group. The image respects whether items are expanded or collapsed.
💾 Saving progress
Names, currency and restaurant are saved automatically and will still be there if you close or refresh the browser — handy if you're eating with the same group all week.
